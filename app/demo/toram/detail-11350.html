Viewer giveaways are only available when announced during the livestream.
(Please note that some livestreams may not feature giveaways.)

Keyword Input

Only players who correctly enter the "keyword" announced during the livestream via in-game chat in the designated area during the specified period will be eligible.

Keyword Entry Confirmation
